Lead story — Strait of Hormuz

Tankers Hit by Mines in the Strait of Hormuz as Gulf War Widens

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard said two oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, as the war in the Gulf spread to the world’s busiest oil waterway. The blast came the same day Yemen’s Houthis said they had attacked Saudi Arabia’s largest refinery, Ras Tanura, days after a drone strike on a Riyadh tower wounded 11 people.

Ukraine · Russia

Kyiv Hits Two Refineries and Five Warplanes Deep Inside Russia

Ukraine said its drones set fire to the Syzran and Kuibyshev oil refineries in Russia’s Samara region overnight and struck Savasleyka airbase, hitting three MiG-31s and two Su-34 jets.

Gaza

Israeli Strike Kills Hamas Security Chief in Gaza City

An Israeli strike on an apartment in the Shati refugee camp killed Mohammad Abu al-Ouf, head of Hamas’s internal security in northern Gaza, along with his wife and two daughters, Gaza medics said.

United States · Tech

Huawei Convicted on 10 of 12 Counts in US Criminal Trial

A Brooklyn jury found the Chinese tech giant guilty of bank fraud, wire fraud, sanctions evasion and conspiracy to steal trade secrets, but cleared it of the overarching racketeering charge.

Markets

Chip Rout Deepens as Dell Posts Record $54.9bn Quarter

Chip stocks fell again on Wednesday — Nvidia down sharply for a second day — even as Dell reported revenue up 103% and a record $132.3bn AI server backlog. The Dow held above 50,000; US growth was revised up to 4.3%.

Space

Pakistan’s First Astronaut Reaches China’s Space Station

China’s Shenzhou-24 launched on Wednesday and docked with the Tiangong station, carrying Muhammad Zeeshan Ali — the first foreign astronaut ever to visit Tiangong — alongside two Chinese crewmates.

War toll

Leaked Data Puts Russian War Dead at 490,334, BBC Reports

Leaked Russian state statistics analysed by the BBC show at least 490,334 Russian soldiers have died in Ukraine since 2022; researchers say the true toll may approach 700,000.
